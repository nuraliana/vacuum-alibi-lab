## Winner: #2 — 24/25

"The dryer started it. I just finished the job."

Why it wins: it hijacks the one sock joke everyone already knows (dryers eat
socks) and turns the vacuum from culprit into accomplice — a blame-shift no
other field entry uses. Two short deadpan sentences, 46 characters, zero
wasted words; the twist lands in the second sentence ("finished the job"
reads as both housework and a hit). It is the vacuum speaking, it is about
the sock, and it needs no setup.

Avoided angles: stuck under a chair; reclassifying stains as furniture;
firmware remap to edible upholstery/argyle; "tried to fold, no hands"; sock
adopted not eaten; beep twice; sock that never nagged; sock in the trash can.
